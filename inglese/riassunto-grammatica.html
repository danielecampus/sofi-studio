Present Simple vs Present Continuous

Units 2–3

Quando si usa

Present Simple → abitudini, routine, fatti sempre veri, gusti.
Present Continuous → azioni in corso ADESSO o situazioni temporanee.

Forma

 Present SimplePresent Continuous
AffermativaI work / she worksI am working / she is working
NegativaI don't / she doesn't workI 'm not / she isn't working
InterrogativaDo I / Does she work?Am I / Is she working?

Regola chiave: Stative verbs

Alcuni verbi (di stato) non si usano al continuous: like, love, hate, want, need, know, understand, believe, prefer, seem e i verbi di percezione see, hear, taste, smell.

Esempi

  • She usually walks to school. (abitudine)
  • Look! She is walking in the rain. (adesso)
  • I love this book. (NON “I am loving”)
Attenzione: aggiungi -s alla 3ª persona singolare del present simple (he/she/it): he plays. Con -o, -ss, -sh, -ch, -x → -es (go → goes).

✍️ Mettiti alla prova

  1. Every morning my dad ________ (drive) to work.
  2. Listen! The birds ________ (sing) in the garden.
  3. I ________ (not / understand) this exercise.
  4. She usually ________ (wear) jeans, but today she ________ (wear) a skirt.
Soluzioni: 1 drives · 2 are singing · 3 don't understand · 4 wears / is wearing
2

Quantifiers

Unit 4

Countable vs Uncountable

Countable = si contano (an apple → three apples). Uncountable = non si contano (water, money, bread, sugar): sempre singolari.

Quale quantificatore?

Con countable (plurale)Con uncountable
how many?how much?
too many (troppi)too much (troppo)
a few (alcuni)a little (un po')
a lot of / lots of → con entrambi

some / any · too / enough

some = frasi affermative e offerte/richieste · any = negative e domande.
too + aggettivo = troppo (It's too hot). aggettivo + enough = abbastanza (warm enough). not enough = non abbastanza.

Esempi

  • There isn't any sugar, but there are some biscuits.
  • How much water? / How many eggs?
  • This coffee is too hot and it isn't sweet enough.
Attenzione: much in frasi affermative è raro: meglio a lot of (I have a lot of homework, non “much homework”).

✍️ Mettiti alla prova

  1. There aren't ________ eggs in the fridge. (some / any)
  2. How ________ time do we have? (much / many)
  3. I have ________ friends in London. (much / a lot of)
  4. This tea is ________ sweet; I can't drink it. (too / enough)
Soluzioni: 1 any · 2 much · 3 a lot of · 4 too
3

Past Simple

Units 6–7

Quando si usa

Azioni concluse nel passato, spesso con yesterday, last week, in 2020, two days ago.

Forma

FormaRegolari (+ed)Irregolari
AffermativaI playedI went
NegativaI didn't play / go (verbo base!)
InterrogativaDid you play / go?

Regola chiave: spelling regolari

verbo + -ed. Se finisce in -e → +d (like → liked). consonante + y → -ied (study → studied). Consonante finale spesso raddoppia (stop → stopped).

Esempi

  • We watched a film and had pizza. (reg. + irreg.)
  • She didn't come to the party.
  • Did you enjoy the trip?
Errore comune: dopo did/didn't NON si usa il passato: Did you went? ❌ → Did you go? ✅

✍️ Mettiti alla prova

  1. Last weekend we ________ (go) to the mountains.
  2. She ________ (not / watch) the film.
  3. ________ you ________ (buy) the tickets?
  4. They ________ (have) a great time at the party.
Soluzioni: 1 went · 2 didn't watch · 3 Did … buy · 4 had

Comparativi e Superlativi

Unit 8

Forma

AggettivoComparativo (di più)Superlativo (il più)
corti (tall, fast)taller thanthe tallest
lunghi (modern)more modern thanthe most modern
in -y (easy, happy)easier thanthe easiest
irregolarigood→better, bad→worsethe best, the worst

Regola chiave: as … as

as + aggettivo + as = uguaglianza (tanto…quanto): She is as tall as her brother. Negativo: not as … as = meno di.

Esempi

  • A plane is faster than a train.
  • This is the most beautiful beach in Italy.
  • Today is as sunny as yesterday.
Errore comune: mai due comparativi insieme: more taller ❌ → taller ✅. E dopo il comparativo si usa than, non “that”.

✍️ Mettiti alla prova

  1. A cheetah is ________ (fast) than a lion.
  2. This is ________ (interesting) book I've ever read.
  3. My bag isn't ________ heavy ________ yours. (as … as)
  4. Winter is ________ (bad) season for me.
Soluzioni: 1 faster · 2 the most interesting · 3 as … as · 4 the worst

Il Futuro: will / going to / present continuous

Units 9–10

Quale futuro?

FormaQuando
will + basedecisioni sul momento, previsioni, promesse, offerte
be going to + basepiani/intenzioni già decisi, prove evidenti
present continuousappuntamenti fissati (con orario/luogo)

Regola chiave

will spesso con I think, probably, maybe, I'm sure. going to quando c'è già un piano o una prova (dark clouds → it's going to rain).

Esempi

  • The bag is heavy. I 'll carry it for you. (decisione ora)
  • We are going to paint the house next week. (piano)
  • I 'm meeting Sofia at 6 p.m. (appuntamento)
Attenzione: forma di going to = am/is/are + going to + verbo base. Non dimenticare il verbo be: I going to… ❌

✍️ Mettiti alla prova

  1. Look at the sky! It ________ (rain) soon.
  2. I'm tired. I think I ________ (go) to bed.
  3. We ________ (meet) Anna at 5 — it's arranged.
  4. A: The phone's ringing. B: I ________ (answer) it.
Soluzioni: 1 is going to rain · 2 will go · 3 are meeting · 4 will answer

Zero & First Conditional

Unit 10

Forma

TipoStrutturaUso
Zeroif + present, presentverità / leggi generali
Firstif + present, will + basefuturo possibile e reale

Regola chiave: niente “will” dopo if

Dopo if, when, as soon as, until si usa il presente, mai il will: If it rains, we will stay home.

Esempi

  • If you mix blue and yellow, you get green. (zero)
  • If I study, I 'll pass the test. (first)
  • I'll text you as soon as I arrive.
Errore comune: If it will rain… ❌ → If it rains… ✅. Le due parti possono invertirsi: We'll stay home if it rains.

✍️ Mettiti alla prova

  1. If you ________ (not / hurry), you'll miss the bus.
  2. Ice ________ (melt) if you leave it in the sun.
  3. If it's sunny tomorrow, we ________ (go) to the beach.
  4. I'll call you as soon as I ________ (arrive).
Soluzioni: 1 don't hurry · 2 melts · 3 will go · 4 arrive

Present Perfect + ever/never · been vs gone

Unit 11

Forma

FormaEsempio
AffermativaI have / she has + past participle (3ª colonna)
NegativaI haven't / she hasn't been…
InterrogativaHave you ever been…?

Regola chiave: been vs gone

been to = andato e tornato (esperienza): I've been to London.
gone to = andato e ancora là: She's gone to the shops (non è tornata).

Esempi

  • I have never tried sushi. (mai in vita mia)
  • Have you ever been to Spain?
  • Mum isn't home: she has gone to work.
Attenzione: il present perfect si usa senza dire quando. Se indichi un tempo passato preciso, usa il Past Simple (vedi capitolo 8).

✍️ Mettiti alla prova

  1. I ________ (never / eat) sushi.
  2. ________ you ever ________ (be) to New York?
  3. She's not here — she ________ (go) to the gym.
  4. We ________ (be) to that museum twice.
Soluzioni: 1 have never eaten · 2 Have … been · 3 has gone · 4 have been

Present Perfect (just/already/yet) vs Past Simple

Unit 12

just / already / yet

ParolaSignificatoPosizione
justappenahave just done
alreadygià (affermativa)have already done
yetancora / già (neg. e domande)…done yet? (a fine frase)

Regola chiave: quale tempo?

Present Perfect → tempo non finito o non precisato (today, this week, ever, never).
Past Simple → tempo finito e preciso (yesterday, last year, in 2019, ago).

Esempi

  • I've just finished my homework. (appena)
  • Have you eaten yet? – No, not yet.
  • We visited Rome in 2019. (past simple)
Errore comune: I have seen him yesterday ❌ → I saw him yesterday ✅ (tempo preciso = Past Simple).

✍️ Mettiti alla prova

  1. I've ________ finished my homework! (appena)
  2. Have you called him ________? (ancora)
  3. She ________ (already / see) that film.
  4. We ________ (meet) them last summer.
Soluzioni: 1 just · 2 yet · 3 has already seen · 4 met

Possessivi, pronomi & was/were

Unit 5

Aggettivi e pronomi possessivi

Aggettivo (+ nome)Pronome (da solo)
my, your, his, her, its, our, theirmine, yours, his, hers, —, ours, theirs

Regole chiave

whose = di chi (possesso) · who's = who is / who has.
Double genitive: a friend of mine = un mio amico.
Infinitive of purpose: to + verbo = “per” (I went out to buy milk).
was/were = passato di be: I/he/she/it was — you/we/they were.

Esempi

  • Whose jacket is this? It's hers.
  • He's a cousin of mine.
  • Where were you? I was at the gym.
  • I called to say thank you. (infinito di scopo)
Errore comune: non confondere who's (who is) con whose (di chi): Whose book? ✅ · Who's there? ✅

✍️ Mettiti alla prova

  1. ________ umbrella is this? It's ________. (di chi… / di lei)
  2. They ________ (be) at the cinema yesterday.
  3. He's an old friend ________. (di me)
  4. I went out ________ buy some bread. (per)
Soluzioni: 1 Whose … hers · 2 were · 3 of mine · 4 to
