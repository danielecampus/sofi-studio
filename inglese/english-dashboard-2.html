LIVELLO: VERSO IL B1

9 capitoli di grammatica più impegnativi, presi dal programma del tuo libro (Units 2–12). Leggi le regole, guarda gli esempi e poi prova il quiz alla fine di ogni capitolo. 🧠

🟣 1. Present Simple vs Present Continuous

Units 2–3
Present Simple: abitudini, routine, fatti sempre veri.
Present Continuous: azioni che stanno succedendo ADESSO o situazioni temporanee (am/is/are + verbo-ing).
Stative verbs (like, love, hate, want, know, understand, believe, prefer, need, seem, e i verbi di percezione see, hear, taste, smell) di solito NON si usano al continuous!
  • I go to school by bus. (abitudine)
  • Look! The bus is coming. (adesso)
  • She usually wears jeans, but today she is wearing a dress. (abitudine vs adesso)
  • I love this song. (NON "I am loving")

🔵 2. Quantifiers

Unit 4
Countable (si contano: apple → apples) vs uncountable (non si contano: water, money, bread).
some = frasi affermative · any = negative e domande.
Con countable (plurale)Con uncountable
how many? · too many · a fewhow much? · too much · a little
a lot of / lots of → con entrambi
too / enough: too much/many = troppo · not enough = non abbastanza · too + aggettivo = troppo (It's too hot) · adjective + enough = abbastanza (It's warm enough).
  • There isn't any milk. (Non c'è latte.)
  • How much sugar do you want? (uncountable)
  • There are too many cars. (troppe macchine)
  • I don't have enough money. (non abbastanza soldi)

🟡 3. Past Simple

Units 6–7
Quando: azioni concluse nel passato (spesso con yesterday, last week, in 2020, ago).
Regolari: verbo + -ed · Irregolari: forma propria (go → went).
FormaEsempio
AffermativaShe played / went.
NegativaShe didn't play / go. (verbo base!)
InterrogativaDid she play / go?
Attenzione: dopo didn't e did il verbo torna alla forma base: Did you went? ❌ → Did you go? ✅
  • We watched a film last night. (regolare)
  • He bought a new phone. (irregolare)
  • Did you see the match? (domanda)

🟢 4. Comparatives & Superlatives

Unit 8
AggettivoComparativoSuperlativo
corto (tall)taller thanthe tallest
lungo (modern)more modern thanthe most modern
-y (easy)easier thanthe easiest
irregolarigood→better · bad→worsethe best · the worst
as … as = uguaglianza: She is as tall as me. (alta quanto me)
  • A train is faster than a bike. (più veloce di)
  • Everest is the highest mountain. (la più alta)
  • This exercise is more difficult than the last one.

🔴 5. Il Futuro: will / going to / present continuous

Units 9–10
will → decisioni sul momento, previsioni, promesse, offerte. (I'll help you!)
be going to → piani/intenzioni già decisi, prove evidenti. (Look at those clouds! It's going to rain.)
present continuous → appuntamenti fissati, con orario/luogo. (I'm meeting Sofia at 5.)
  • The phone is ringing. I 'll answer it. (decisione ora)
  • We are going to visit Rome next month. (piano deciso)
  • I 'm seeing the dentist at 3 tomorrow. (appuntamento)

🟠 6. Zero & First Conditional

Unit 10
TipoStrutturaUso
Zeroif + present, presentverità/leggi generali
Firstif + present, will + basefuturo possibile/reale
Dopo if, when, as soon as si usa il presente, mai il will:
If it will rain… ❌ → If it rains… ✅
  • If you heat ice, it melts. (zero – legge)
  • If it rains, we 'll stay at home. (first)
  • I'll call you as soon as I get home.

🟣 7. Present Perfect + ever / never · been vs gone

Unit 11
Forma: have / has + past participle (3ª colonna dei verbi).
Uso: esperienze di vita (senza dire quando), azioni che continuano fino ad ora.
been = andato e tornato (esperienza): I've been to London.
gone = andato e ancora là: She's gone to the shops.
  • I have never eaten sushi. (mai in vita mia)
  • Have you ever been to Paris? (sei mai stata…?)
  • He has gone to school. (è a scuola ora)

🔵 8. Present Perfect (just/already/yet) vs Past Simple

Unit 12
just = appena (I've just arrived) · already = già (affermativa) · yet = ancora/già (in negative e domande, a fine frase).
Present Perfect → tempo NON finito o non detto (today, this week, ever).
Past Simple → tempo finito e preciso (yesterday, last year, in 2020, ago).
  • I've just finished. (appena)
  • Have you done it yet? – No, not yet. (ancora)
  • We went to Spain last year. (past simple – tempo finito)

🟡 9. Possessivi, pronomi & was/were

Unit 5
Aggettivo poss.Pronome poss.
my, your, his, her, our, theirmine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs
whose = di chi (possesso) · who's = who is / who has.
Double genitive: a friend of mine (un mio amico).
Infinitive of purpose: to + verbo = per (I went out to buy bread).
was/were = passato di be (I/he/she/it was; you/we/they were).
  • Whose pen is this? It's hers. (di chi… è suo)
  • He is a friend of mine. (un mio amico)
  • Where were you? I was at home.
